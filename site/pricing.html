Questions we get a lot

How do payments work?

Half when we start and half at launch. For Main Street projects we can split it into monthly payments instead.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain, the content and the code are yours. If you ever want to move, we'll hand everything over.

Do I have to host with you?

No. Most clients do because it's one less account to manage, but we're happy to deploy your site wherever you'd like.

I already have a domain and email. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Dave will connect your existing domain and keep your email working through the switch.

What if I need changes after launch?

Care and Partner plans include edit time every month. Without a plan, small changes are billed hourly and we quote them first.

Tell us what you're working with.

Send us your current site, or just the idea. We'll reply within one business day with what we'd change and a rough price.
