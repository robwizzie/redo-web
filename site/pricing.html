Questions

Pricing FAQ

How do payments work?

50% to start and 50% at launch. For Main Street projects we can split it into monthly payments instead.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your code belong to you. If you ever want to move, we'll hand everything over.

Do I have to host with you?

No, but most clients do because it means one less account to manage. If you'd rather host elsewhere, we'll deploy it there for you.

I already have a domain and email. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Dave will connect your existing domain and keep your email working through the switch.

What if I need changes after launch?

Care and Partner plans include monthly edit time. Without a plan, small changes are billed hourly and quoted first.
