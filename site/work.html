Trouble Brewing Coffee House

An independent coffee shop in Haddon Heights, NJ. The site has a chalkboard of today's specials, a live open-now board and a menu synced from their SpotOn point of sale. Their top-hatted fox mascot is animated and answers visitor questions about hours, parking and dog-friendly seating.

The owners update the menu, events, hours, reviews and photos themselves from an admin panel, with no Squarespace bill. If the backend ever goes down, the site falls back to built-in content so it never looks broken.

Mile A Day

It started as a bet between two friends to run a mile every day. The streak hit 368 days and became a free iPhone and Apple Watch app. Miles are detected automatically from Apple Health, and you can race friends, earn medals and keep Flamey, your streak mascot, lit.

Rob and Dave co-founded it and built all of it together: the app, the API it runs on and the marketing site at mileaday.run. It's the same team that will build your site.
