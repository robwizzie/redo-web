Trouble Brewing Coffee House

A neighborhood coffee shop with a ton of personality and a website that had none. We gave them a site that feels like walking in the door, and the tools to run it themselves.

troublebrewingcoffeehouse.com

The brief

Their old site was a Wix page that had fallen out of date. Hours were wrong, the menu was a PDF, and nothing about it felt like the shop.

Now the menu, specials, hours, events and reviews all live in one place the owners update from their phone, and the site never looks broken. If the backend goes down, it falls back to built-in content.

What we built

  • Chalkboard specials"On the board today" updates in seconds from the admin panel.
  • Live open-now boardHours that are always right, with an open or closed badge.
  • Menu synced from SpotOnPrices match the register, with Order Now on every page.
  • The Trouble foxAn animated mascot that answers questions about hours, parking and more.
  • Owner admin panelMenu, events, hours, reviews, photos and polls, with no Squarespace bill.
  • Real reviewsGoogle reviews and guests' own photos, on a gallery wall.

The brief

They needed a brand site that could also sell. Every bottle got its own page with ingredients and color, and customers can order for pickup or delivery and pay online.

Order emails go straight to the owners, and they can switch online ordering off when they sell out.

What we built

  • A page for every flavorTropical Storm to Midnight Rush, each with its own colors and ingredients.
  • Stripe checkoutA five-step order flow: location, delivery, date, contact and payment.
  • Swipeable juice carouselDrag or tap through the lineup on the homepage.
  • Order emailsNew orders land in the owners' inbox with everything they need.
  • Athlete partnersA page for the athletes who drink and promote the juice.
  • Ordering on/off switchPause online orders when the batch sells out.

Our own app · iPhone, Apple Watch and web Live

Mile A Day

It started as a bet between Rob and Dave to run a mile every day. Nobody lost. The streak hit 368 days and became a free app with a real community behind it.

mileaday.runApp Store

Everything: app, API, website, merch store
Built with

Platforms
iPhone, Apple Watch, widgets, web
Status

mileaday.run

The brief

This is the project that proves the two of us can ship something big together. Rob built the app and website, and Dave built the backend it runs on.

Miles are picked up automatically from Apple Health, friends race each other, and Flamey the streak mascot celebrates every mile.

What we built

  • Automatic mile detectionReads Apple Health and Apple Watch, with no manual logging.
  • Friends and competitionsHead-to-head races, ghost runs and leaderboards.
  • Medals and Flamey's closetBadges unlock outfits and colors for the mascot.
  • Share StudioInstagram-ready streak cards and stickers.
  • WidgetsHome and lock screen widgets keep your streak in view.
  • Live community statsThe website shows real miles and members as they happen.
