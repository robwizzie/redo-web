Launch day

What's done before we call it finished.

Every build ends with the same checklist, whatever the package. If something on it isn't done, the site isn't live yet.

  • Designed for your businessEvery page laid out from scratch around your brand. No themes.
  • Tested on real phonesiPhone, Android and a couple of old laptops, not just a resized browser.
  • Domain and SSL connectedYour address works with and without www, and the padlock shows.
  • Forms that reach youEvery contact form is tested end to end and copies land in a backup inbox.
  • Google can find youPage titles, descriptions and your Google Business Profile all point to the new site.
  • Fast on a slow connectionWe check page speed before launch and aim for a Lighthouse score of 90 or more.
  • Analytics you can readA simple dashboard showing visits and where they came from.
  • Backups switched onDaily copies of your site, kept for 30 days.
  • A walkthrough with usA 30-minute call showing you how to update the things you'll change most.

How a project runs

About four weeks from first call to launch.

  1. Week 0

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes on your business and your customers. A fixed quote follows within two days.

  2. Weeks 1–2

    Design

    We design the key pages and send a clickable preview. Two rounds of changes are included.

  3. Weeks 2–4

    Build

    The site goes up on a private link you can check from your phone at any point.

  4. Launch

    Go live

    Dave points your domain at the new site and we watch it closely for the first week.
