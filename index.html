04 · Pricing

Suggested starting prices

Set to undercut agencies ($8k–$25k for similar work) while staying above DIY builders, so the price signals custom work. Raise prices after Trouble Brewing launches and the portfolio grows.

Main Street Program (no public price) For local shops, new businesses and nonprofits. The site says "tell us your budget" and the contact form has a "Small business: let's talk" option. Offer payment plans over 3–6 months, a smaller first version (e.g. one great page) and reduced hosting. A suggested floor is around $600, kept internal.
05 · Before launch

Open items

BothDomain

Everything uses revampweb.com as a placeholder. Check that it's available, or pick another, before printing cards.

RobReal screenshots

Work cards use drawn stand-ins. Swap in real screenshots of Trouble Brewing, the juice company (and its real name) and your portfolio.

DaveContact form backend

The form is front-end only. It needs an endpoint that emails you both and stores leads, plus spam protection.

DaveHosting stack

Pick where client sites live, how deploys work and how backups and uptime checks run. The Care plan prices assume this is mostly automated.

BothCard details

Add Dave's last name and real phone numbers in the card file, then re-run the export script.

BothTestimonials

Ask Trouble Brewing for a quote after launch. A real testimonial will do more for the homepage than anything else.
